    Subjects

    Add or edit a subject. Deactivate a subject that already has records. Delete is only available when nothing refers to it.

    Examination types

    Only FAT, Assignment, and End Semester Examination are permitted. CAT scores, assignment scores, and the external mark are saved in the assessment form. They are not stored again here.

    FAT is the formative assessment. The three CAT scores stay in the internal assessment.

    Internal assessment and external examination

    Enter whole numbers only. Leave a box blank when that score has not been recorded. Blank is not zero. CAT and assignment scores are out of 50. The external raw mark is out of 100.

    Earlier examination record

    These rows are the older examination scores. The official Semester 1 grade is calculated from the assessment above.
