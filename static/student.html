Your Study Plan

A study plan is a personal list of what to review. You can generate a suggestion from your results, change the wording, and save your own version.

The plan is editable guidance. It is not an official academic result, and opening this page does not change a saved plan.

No study plan yet. Generate a suggested plan based on your academic results and reported difficulties, then review and save it.

1. Generate a suggested study plan

This suggestion uses your stored marks and the difficulties you reported. It appears in the box below so you can review it. It is not saved until you save it.

2. Edit or save my study plan

Change the text to match how you want to study, then save. The success message appears only after the saved text is read back from the database.

3. Explain my study plan with AI

The local assistant can clarify the plan in the box, explain difficult topics, and suggest how to approach each activity. The answer is guidance, not an official result.

Revision Suggestions

This recommends subjects and topics to revise from your stored marks and the difficulties you reported. It also lists learning resources already in the database.

Official marks stay as recorded. The assistant explains them and suggests activities. It does not decide a pass or fail.
